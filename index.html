03 · My Philosophy

AI is a capability inside a system I design and control.

I don't start by asking what AI can do. I start by asking what the problem requires.

Then I decide what the system should do, where intelligence belongs, what should stay deterministic, what data AI can reach, and how its output gets checked.

I use AI seriously, and it makes me faster at implementation, exploration and problem solving. It doesn’t replace understanding the code, the architecture, the infrastructure, the data, the security boundaries, the system’s behaviour or how its agents interact. That understanding, and the ownership that comes with it, stays with me.

AI can be part of the solution. It is never the solution by itself. The technology serves the problem, the system serves the product, and every important decision still passes through engineering judgment.

AI doesn't build the product. I do.
